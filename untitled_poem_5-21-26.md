# Untitled Poem 5-21-26

Sometimes I wish things could just be,<br>
like those quiet intimate moments between bee and flower,<br>
like a sun-baked hound in the yard of its domicile.<br> 

But the restless noise crescendoes,<br>
the racket of thought crashes on the scene and institutes<br>
its chaotic reign.<br>

So, you say, "Pass the tea."<br>
And we convene<br>
For boredom breeds intrigue,<br> 
holding at bay the silence of being.<br>

Or is that the muted echo<br> 
For which being is a vessel<br>
Discerned sweetly in repose<br>
Or with sharp-angled ear?<br>

I accept you as lovely<br>
Diversion, as the playing out<br>
Of scales,<br>
Of varied human melody.<br>

We sit,
Held by being,
Carried along by this fragile vessel,
Keeping company.
